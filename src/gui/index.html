Separate a pre-fork coin

A coin you held before the fork (block 961,632, 30 Aug 2026) exists on both chains — BTC (SHA256) and BTC (Blake2b) — under the same key. A spend of it on one chain can be copied ("replayed") onto the other and move the twin without your intent. This tool moves the coin once, on the BTC (Blake2b) chain, in a way that can exist on that chain only — so the two copies become independent and safe.

Do you even need this? If you sign with a BTC (Blake2b) wallet (Shrike), your spends are already safe — just spend the coin normally, for free. This tool is for a coin you can only move with a regular Bitcoin wallet, hardware wallet, Sparrow or Electrum: those sign the old way, which is valid on both chains, so you need the marker.

We lend a BTC (Blake2b)-only "marker" coin and recover it; you sign on your own device (non-custodial), we only co-sign the marker. Fee 0.5% of the coin, taken in BTC (Blake2b). How it works →

1. Your coin and where it should go

Only one address is needed. Your BTC (SHA256) coin is not moved — it stays put and becomes safe to spend afterwards.

3. Sign it on your own device

Copy this unsigned transaction, sign it in your wallet / signer, then paste the signed result back below. We never see your keys.

4. Confirm it is safe

Wait for 12 confirmations on BTC (Blake2b), then verify the coin can never be replayed before you spend the BTC (SHA256) side.

Safety

  • Never move or consolidate the coin on the BTC (SHA256) side first — that move is replayable.
  • The separating move always includes our BTC (Blake2b) marker (the builder enforces it).
  • Wait at least 12 BTC (Blake2b) confirmations before trusting it (this low-hashrate chain reorgs).
  • Only when verify says FINAL is it safe to spend the BTC (SHA256) side.
