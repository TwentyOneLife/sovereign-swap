Separate a pre-fork coin

A coin you held at block 961,631 or earlier exists on both chains under the same key. Until you separate it, a move you make on one chain can be copied ("replayed") onto the other.

Signing with a Blake2b wallet (Shrike)? Its signatures are rejected by the SHA256 chain at consensus, so a spend you make there is already non-replayable - you do not need this tool. Get Shrike for StartOS: TwentyOneLife/shrike-startos.

This is for a coin you must move with a stock / legacy signer, or when you want a guarantee independent of your signer. We lend a Blake2b-only "marker" coin (and recover it): the move cannot exist on the SHA256 chain because that input does not exist there. Fee 0.5%, in XBT. Non-custodial - you sign on your own device; we only co-sign our marker.

1. Your coin

2. Sign it

Sign this unsigned PSBT on your own device/signer, then paste the signed PSBT back.

3. Confirm it is safe

Wait for 12 confirmations on the Blake2b chain, then verify the coin can never be replayed before you spend the SHA256 side.

Safety

  • Never move or consolidate the coin on the SHA256 side first - that move is replayable.
  • The separating move must include our Blake2b marker (the builder enforces it).
  • Wait at least 12 Blake2b confirmations before trusting it (this low-hashrate chain reorgs).
  • Only when verify says FINAL is it safe to spend the SHA256 side.
