Protect your BTC (Blake2b) coins

Do you hold Bitcoin from before the fork (block 961,632, on 30 Aug 2026)? Each of those coins also exists on the BTC (Blake2b) chain under the same key. Until you separate the two, a spend on one chain can be copied ("replayed") onto the other. This service separates them safely, so your BTC (Blake2b) coins become yours alone and can never be replayed.

What you need. A BTC (Blake2b) wallet to receive the separated coins: use Shrike (desktop app, or on StartOS). Your existing BTC (SHA256) wallet, for example Sparrow, holds the pre-fork coin and signs the move. Your BTC (SHA256) coin itself is never moved: it stays exactly where it is and simply becomes safe to spend.

We lend a BTC (Blake2b) "marker" coin and take it back, so the move cannot exist on the BTC (SHA256) chain. You sign on your own device (non-custodial); we only co-sign the marker. Fee 0.5% of the coin, taken in BTC (Blake2b). Learn how it works.

1. Your coin, and where to send it

Advanced: use one specific coin instead of an address

You only need one new address, the BTC (Blake2b) destination. Your BTC (SHA256) coin is not moved; it stays put and is safe to spend afterwards.

3. Sign it on your own device

Open this unsigned transaction in Sparrow (or your signer), sign it, and paste the signed result back below. We never see your keys.

4. Confirm it is safe

Wait for 12 confirmations on BTC (Blake2b), then verify the coin can never be replayed before you spend the BTC (SHA256) side.

Safety

  • Never move or consolidate the coin on the BTC (SHA256) side first; that move is replayable.
  • The separating move always includes our BTC (Blake2b) marker (the builder enforces it).
  • Wait at least 12 BTC (Blake2b) confirmations before trusting it (this low-hashrate chain reorgs).
  • Only when verify says FINAL is it safe to spend the BTC (SHA256) side.
